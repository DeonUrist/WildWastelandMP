Activation repair (2026-10-18): static inspection of the installed Vistas and MonoMod.Utils DLLs established a field-signature collision. Vistas' `a.r` has seven distinct fields named `A`, including Single, Single[], Boolean, Vector3 and its own structs; `A.c` likewise overloads `A` as Boolean, Int32 and String. MonoMod's `_ResolveReflection` enumerates fields and uses Extensions.Is(MemberReference, MemberInfo), which checks the name/declaring type but never compares a FieldReference's field type. Harmony's ILManipulator uses that resolver while preparing every field operand, including fields from the original method. Correct WW target lookup alone therefore cannot fix the copied method.

The repair adds a Core prefix scoped to field references from the loaded Vistas assembly only. It resolves the declaring type and full field type, then selects the matching field, avoiding ambiguous name-only resolution while preserving the original Vistas code and all non-Vistas resolution. Constructed generic field types are compared as resolved System.Type instances; required/optional modifier wrappers are unwrapped as reflection FieldType does. No external DLL edit, dependency/version change, config entry, gameplay job or scene scan.

## Causes and changes for the requested items

1. **Sandstorms:** Vistas has a separate global dust-storm/haboob weather rotation, screen veil, dust front, particles and forces. While `Services.Weather` is present, its dust eligibility and haboob eligibility return false; explicit dust/haboob transitions become ordinary windy weather. Clear/haze/wind/clouds and the other special weather remain with Vistas. WW retains the native storm encounters, region geometry, hazards, palette, audio and camera-scoped Azure fog/lighting restoration. This is an ownership choice to prevent two independent sandstorms, not a merge of Vistas weather into WW.
2. **Biome/landmark names:** the source actually documents biome arrival as journal-only, without a popup. Its existing debounced place-entry callback now supplies a five-second CleanHUD atlas/accent banner; successful nearby landmark discoveries use it too. `Regions.BannerHeight` still applies. Native landmark banner drawing is suppressed only while Clean HUD is enabled; journal writing and proximity discovery remain original.
3. **Debug overlay:** the source `Debug.Overlay` entry is set false on each WW startup, including an old saved true. It can still be deliberately enabled later in Vistas settings. WW does not disable Vistas or its non-debug hot-engine warning.
4. **Height discovery:** Vistas summit scouting and far-landmark registration are suppressed. WW samples the terrain directly below and eight surrounding points 1 km away, one probe per 0.2-second tick. Prominence/height produces a 1–3x range multiplier (full bonus at 300 m); base radial fog reveal caps at 4 km, with existing 1.5x scope/2x binocular cone multipliers applied afterward. Location/traffic compass range is 1 km at ground level, up to 3 km high up. Visible targets must be in the camera view and pass nonalloc obstruction checks before their ambiguity clears; normal 100 m visits and binocular identification remain. Location discoveries and fog keep existing save formats; traffic identity keeps its existing journey lifetime. Fog rows paint in a 500 us slice so the expanded radius does not become one large synchronous pass.
5. **Vehicles:** original routing penalised steep ground, sampled only eight points along very long legs, and fell back to an unchecked snake when a search failed. Sources/destinations were random. Vistas Replace mode also empties the original road splines, so those hints cannot identify its new roads.

   The bridge snapshots Vistas' real generated centreline segments. Vehicle routing uses a bounded worker A* search, snaps road candidates onto those lines, prefers roads and low slopes, checks intermediate ground every 10 m and a 6 m wide corridor, and rejects grades above 22% for convoys/trucks or 30% for other cars (about 12.4° / 16.7°). No unknown/steep-ground fallback is accepted. A 500 us slice captures 257x257 heights from applied main terrain, including after Vistas' own grading callback. The regenerative road cache is v3 and includes the Vistas DLL/settings identity; no save schema is renamed. Up to four source/four destination candidates are compared, with full searches for the six most promising pairs. Reinforcement origins and roaming destinations use the same preference. Physical materialization additionally checks the actual terrain at the centre and four chassis-footprint points, with a stricter truck footprint. Saved remaining routes revalidate before travel/materialization; their intermediate stops are retained. Foot routes keep their existing solver and limits.

   A safe route may be deferred while terrain is missing or still being captured. This deliberately permits fewer vehicle departures rather than forcing one across a ridge. The strategic search and coarse height grid cannot establish actual driving success or every small obstacle: native physical clearance remains the final spawn check, and Denis must test road following, heavy trucks, cliffs and runtime cost. Combat pursuits still use the existing AI behavior.
